257 Franklin Street Records Tracker

Following public-records requests about the environmental condition of a proposed Greenpoint elementary school site.

New York City plans a 450-seat elementary school at 257 Franklin Street in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, beside the Former NuHart Plastic Manufacturing State Superfund site. The state certified the NuHart cleanup complete in July 2025, with contamination remaining under long-term management. This page tracks Freedom of Information Law requests for the test results behind those decisions, and posts the records as they arrive.

To: Records Access Officer New York City School Construction Authority 30-30 Thomson Avenue Long Island City, NY 11101 Re: Freedom of Information Law request: environmental records for 257 Franklin Street, Brooklyn (proposed elementary school) Dear Records Access Officer, Under the New York Freedom of Information Law, Article 6 of the Public Officers Law, I request copies of the following records concerning the property at 257 Franklin Street, Brooklyn, NY 11222, the site of a proposed 450-seat elementary school. The request covers records from January 1, 2019 to the date of your response. 1. Every Phase I Environmental Site Assessment and Phase II Environmental Site Investigation report for the property, complete with figures, sample location maps, analytical data tables, laboratory reports, and boring and monitoring well logs. 2. For all soil vapor, sub-slab vapor, indoor air and ambient air samples collected at or next to the property: the sample locations, depths, dates, collection methods and analytical results. 3. Records of any environmental sampling carried out at the property after the Phase II investigation, including groundwater, soil and soil vapor results. 4. Any remedial investigation report, remedial action work plan or site management plan prepared for the property, and the design documents for the vapor barrier and sub-slab depressurization system planned for the school. 5. Applications to, and correspondence with, the New York City Mayor's Office of Environmental Remediation about enrolling the property in the City Voluntary Cleanup Program. 6. Correspondence with the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation and the New York State Department of Health about the property and its relationship to the Former NuHart Plastic Manufacturing site (State Superfund Site No. 224136). 7. The environmental review prepared for the selection of this site under the State Environmental Quality Review Act, including any environmental assessment and determination of significance. 8. Presentation materials on the environmental condition of the property given to Brooklyn Community Board 1 or to elected officials. 9. Records identifying the architect and engineers engaged to design the school, including the design contract or task order and its scope of work as it relates to outdoor play space and environmental controls. I ask for the records in electronic form, by email or download link. If any fee would exceed $25, please tell me before proceeding. Sample results, laboratory reports, data tables and location maps are factual data. Under Public Officers Law section 87(2)(g)(i) they must be disclosed even where they appear in draft or consultant-prepared documents. If you withhold any record or part of a record, please cite the specific exemption relied on, release the remainder, and tell me how to appeal. Section 89(3)(a) requires a response within five business days of receipt. If the records cannot be provided in that time, please give me the date by which they will be. Thank you for your help.
